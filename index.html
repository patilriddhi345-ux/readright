Read Right:

Student Awareness on Digital and Print Reading Practices

This project promotes awareness about balanced reading habits using both digital and printed materials for effective learning.

Introduction

Reading is a fundamental learning activity. In today’s world, students use both e-books and printed books, each offering unique benefits.

E-Books (Digital Reading)

E-books provide instant access, portability, search features, and interactive content. They are ideal for quick reference, online learning, and remote education.

Tap to Open E-Book

Printed Books (Traditional Reading)

Printed books improve focus, reduce screen fatigue, and support deep reading and long-term comprehension.

Objectives

  • Promote awareness of e-books and printed books
  • Encourage healthy screen usage
  • Improve reading comprehension
  • Support balanced learning habits

The Swing

By Robert Louis Stevenson

Up in the air so blue?
Oh, I do think it the pleasantest thing

Till I can see so wide,
River and trees and cattle and all

Down on the roof so brown—
