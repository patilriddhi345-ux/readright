Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is Read Right?
Read Right is a student awareness project focused on healthy reading habits.

2. Are e-books better than printed books?
Both have benefits. E-books offer convenience, while printed books improve focus.

3. Do e-books harm eyesight?
Long screen time may cause eye strain if breaks are not taken.

4. Why are printed books important?
They help with deep reading, memory retention, and concentration.

5. When should students use e-books?
For quick reference and portability.

6. What is the best reading method?
A hybrid approach using both digital and print formats.

7. How much screen time is safe?
Follow the 20-20-20 rule.

8. How can reading habits be improved?
Read daily and avoid distractions.

9. Who can use this chatbot?
Students and teachers.

10. What can this chatbot help with?
Reading tips and study guidance.
